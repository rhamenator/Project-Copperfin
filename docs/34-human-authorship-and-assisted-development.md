## Human-Control Evidence

The durable repository record should preserve evidence of human creative and
engineering control, including:

- owner-authored requirements, issues, architecture, and acceptance criteria;
- selection and arrangement of components and interfaces;
- review, rejection, correction, and revision of proposed changes;
- compatibility research and clean-room evidence choices;
- tests selected or created to determine acceptable behavior;
- commit, review, issue, and release decisions; and
- integration of individual changes into one coherent platform.

under the applicable law.

## Review And Provenance Rule

Assisted output is untrusted proposed material until a human reviews and
accepts it. Review must check behavior, provenance, restricted-source risk,
third-party similarity, licensing, security, and compatibility. Material that
appears copied, cannot be explained, or cannot be licensed must not enter the
product. Human modification, selection, arrangement, and integration should be
preserved in ordinary Git, issue, review, test, and documentation history.

Contributors remain responsible for the right-to-submit certification in
`CONTRIBUTING.md`, regardless of which tools they used. This policy documents
provenance practice; it does not expand anyone's copyright or replace legal
requirements in a contributor's jurisdiction.
