# Contributor Credit

Project Copperfin preserves contributor credit through the repository's
durable development record:

- Git commit authorship and consenting `Co-authored-by` metadata;
- pull-request and issue authorship and review history; and
- GitHub's contributor history derived from merged commits.

Contributors retain copyright in their contributions. The repository does not
require copyright assignment and does not maintain a hand-edited roster that
could become incomplete or incorrectly attribute work.

Generated or automated assistance does not displace the human contributors
responsible for a change. Preserve original authorship when modifying or
carrying work forward. Authorship corrections should be requested before
merge when possible, or through the documentation-feedback issue form.

The licensing terms for contributions are in [`CONTRIBUTING.md`](CONTRIBUTING.md)
and the operative license is [`LICENSE`](LICENSE).
