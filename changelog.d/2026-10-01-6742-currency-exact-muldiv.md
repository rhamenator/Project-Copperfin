- 2026-10-01: Fixed #6742: Currency multiply and divide are exact beyond 2^53 scaled units (about 900.7 billion units of the currency) on every platform. `$900719925474.0003 * 1`, `/ 1`, `* 1.0001` and the `Number / Currency` forms returned a value off by one or more units in the last digits (and differed between Linux/macOS and Windows, where a `long double` is only 53 bits). They are computed in portable 128-bit integers and rounded half away from zero at the fourth decimal; a Number operand is read as its exact decimal. Results outside the Currency range stay error 1988. This is a deliberate improvement over VFP9, which loses the same digits through a double above that range.
