- 2026-09-25: `CLOSE ALL`, `CLOSE DATABASE`, and `CLOSE DATABASES [ALL]`
  released live native/Automation objects (#6195) and revoked Foxtools
  `RegFn()`/`RegFn32()` registrations (#6198) even though neither is part
  of any `CLOSE` variant's documented VFP9 contract; a program that held a
  form or a registered external-function handle across a `CLOSE` found it
  unusable. `CLOSE DATABASE(S)` also closed outstanding low-level
  `FOPEN`/`FCREATE` handles (#6196), which VFP9 reserves for `CLOSE ALL`
  only. All three are fixed, verified against retained real VFP9 SP2
  probe evidence. `RQ-CF-PRG-CLOSE-SCOPE-001`. `CLOSE DATABASE(S)`/`CLOSE
  TABLES` still close every cursor in the data session instead of only
  DBC-member tables (#6197) — that needs DBC table-membership tracking
  the runtime doesn't have yet and is not part of this fix.
