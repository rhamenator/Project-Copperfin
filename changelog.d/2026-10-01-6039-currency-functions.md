- 2026-10-01: Fixed #6039: `ABS`, `INT`, `CEILING`, `FLOOR`, `ROUND` and `MOD` keep a Currency argument as Currency (exact four-decimal arithmetic) instead of returning a Number, and use VFP9's Currency rules: `INT` and `FLOOR` truncate toward zero and `CEILING` rounds away from zero, so `CEILING($-12.3456)` is `$-13` and `FLOOR($-12.3456)` is `$-12` (the opposite of Numeric); `ROUND` is half away from zero at the requested place; `MOD` takes the divisor's sign and is error 1307 for a zero divisor. `MAX` and `MIN` are Currency only when every argument is. Currency overflow and aggregates follow separately.
