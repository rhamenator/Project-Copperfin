- 2026-09-30: Fixed #6573: `APPEND FROM TYPE CSV` and `TYPE DELIMITED` into a local DBF now round decimal text to the numeric field's scale the way installed VFP9 does (half away from zero, by the digits: `-1.235` becomes `-1.24` in `N(5,2)`, `1.005` becomes `1.01`, `1.9` becomes `2` in `N(3,0)`, and a value that rounds to zero carries no sign). Previously such a value was rejected when its unrounded text was wider than the field, or stored with more decimals than the field declares. A value that is still too wide after rounding keeps the existing rejection and rollback; whether to copy VFP9's silent zero/decimal-dropping there remains the owner decision in #6572.
