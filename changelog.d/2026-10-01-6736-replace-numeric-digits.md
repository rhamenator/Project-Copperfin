- 2026-10-01: Fixed #6736: `REPLACE` into a Numeric, Float or Double field stores the value, rounded to the field's decimals, instead of its six-significant-digit display text. `REPLACE n WITH 123.456789` stored `123.457` and `1.0000005` stored `1` before; they now store `123.456789` and `1.0000005` in a field wide enough, as in VFP9. Other write paths that serialize numbers are not yet audited.
