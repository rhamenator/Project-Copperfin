- 2026-10-01: Fixed #6034: numeric `=`, `<>`, `!=`, `#`, `<`, `>`, `<=`, `>=`, `INLIST`, `ASCAN` and SQL `IN` no longer treat numbers closer than 0.000001 as equal. `1.0000000 = 1.0000005` is `.F.`, `INLIST(1, 1.0000005)` is `.F.` and `ASCAN` does not match, as in VFP9. Numbers one unit in the last place apart still compare equal, so a computed `0.1+0.2 = 0.3` and `4.35*100 = 435` stay `.T.`; integer and Currency comparisons are exact. VFP9 also rounds computed results to the operands' decimal places, which this runtime does not track (`1E14+0.01 = 1E14` is `.F.` in VFP9). `SELECT DISTINCT` is separate (#5905).
