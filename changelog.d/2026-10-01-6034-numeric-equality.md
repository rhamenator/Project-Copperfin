- 2026-10-01: Fixed #6034 and #6736: numeric `=`, `<>`, `!=`, `#`, `<`, `>`, `<=`, `>=`, `INLIST`, `ASCAN` and SQL `IN` no longer treat numbers closer than 0.000001 as equal (`1.0000000 = 1.0000005` is `.F.`, `INLIST(1, 1.0000005)` is `.F.`, `ASCAN` does not match), while numbers one unit in the last place apart still compare equal (`0.1+0.2 = 0.3`, `4.35*100 = 435`) and Integer and Currency comparisons are exact; VFP9 also rounds computed results to the operands' decimal places, which this runtime does not track (`1E14+0.01 = 1E14` is `.F.` in VFP9), and `SELECT DISTINCT`/`GROUP BY` keep their own tolerance (#5905). `REPLACE` into a Numeric, Float or Double field now stores the value, rounded to the field's decimals, instead of its six-significant-digit display text (`REPLACE n WITH 123.456789` stored `123.457` and `1.0000005` stored `1`); `INSERT INTO`, `GATHER`, SQL `UPDATE` and additive `REPLACE` already kept the digits and `APPEND FROM` was not checked.
