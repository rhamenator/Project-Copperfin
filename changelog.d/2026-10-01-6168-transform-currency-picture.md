- 2026-10-01: Fixed #6168: `TRANSFORM()` honors a currency-only numeric picture such as `'$999'`, as VFP9 does. The value is truncated toward zero and right-aligned in the picture's width with the dollar sign taking the leftmost padding space (`TRANSFORM(5,'$999')` is `$  5`, `TRANSFORM(12,'$999')` is `$ 12`); a value that fills the width drops the sign (`1000` is `1000`) and a wider one is asterisks. Floating `$$` pictures, `@C`/`@X` suffixes and blank `@Z` fields are not changed.
