- 2026-10-01: Fixed #5924: `MDY(d)` and `DMY(d)` format a Date or DateTime as English text (`April 18, 2026` and `18 April 2026`), with a two-digit day and a two-digit year when `SET CENTURY` is OFF; an empty value is `*bad date*`. The invented numeric constructors are gone: `MDY(4, 18, 2026)` and `DMY(18, 4, 2026)` are error 1230 "Too many arguments.", no argument is error 1229, and a Character, Numeric, Logical or Object argument is error 11, all catchable with `TRY/CATCH`. To build a date from numbers use `DATE(year, month, day)`.
