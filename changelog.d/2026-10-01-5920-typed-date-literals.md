- 2026-10-01: Fixed #5920: strict date and datetime literals are Date and DateTime values, as in VFP9. `{^2021-01-02}` is a Date and `{^2021-01-02 12:34:56}` a DateTime, so `YEAR`, `MONTH`, `DAY`, `HOUR`, `MINUTE`, `SEC`, `WEEK`, `DOW` and date arithmetic work on them instead of returning zero or concatenating text; `{}` is the empty Date and `{ : }` the empty DateTime. A literal with an out-of-range month, day or time is error 2034 and a `^` literal that does not match `{^yyyy-mm-dd[,][hh[:mm[:ss]] [a|p]]}` is error 2032, both catchable with `TRY/CATCH`. Non-strict braces such as `{06/15/2026}` are still Character text because this runtime has no `SET STRICTDATE`. Date and DateTime values still display datetimes in 24-hour form and empty dates as an empty string (#5998).
