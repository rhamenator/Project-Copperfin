- 2026-10-01: Fixed #6036 and #6061: a `$`-prefixed literal is a Currency value, and a Currency (`Y`) table field reads as Currency. `$1.25`, `$.5`, `$-1` and `$ 5` are `VARTYPE` `Y` (rounded to four decimals; a value outside +-922337203685477.5807 is error 1988) instead of Logical true, and `+`, `-`, `*` and `/` with a Currency operand stay Currency (`$1.25 + 1` is `$2.25`, `$1.25 * $2` is `$2.50`); a `Y` field is no longer a floating-point Number. `TRANSFORM` of a Currency shows the currency symbol, grouped digits and `SET DECIMALS` places (`$1,234.57`) and follows `SET POINT`, `SET SEPARATOR` and `SET CURRENCY`; an existing test expected the symbol to be missing. Malformed literals, the Currency-preserving functions, aggregates and overflow follow separately.
